## 1. Halcyon Nimbus: 3D product launch page
The earbud case opens in real-time 3D the moment the page loads. Scroll, and the earbud separates into seven labelled parts, then clicks back together. Pick a colour and the model changes in your hands.

**Live:** https://halcyon-nimbus.vercel.app · **Watch:** [YouTube](https://youtu.be/Lszv2L23wCw) · **Video file:** [halcyon-nimbus.mp4](halcyon-nimbus.mp4) · **Code:** [halcyon-nimbus-code.html](halcyon-nimbus-code.html)


## 2. Studio EchoFrame: before & after redesign
Drag the slider and watch a tired 2016 template turn into a site that feels like walking into one of the studio's own buildings. The enquiry form drops from 9 fields to 3, the projects sit on the homepage, and a 3D house assembles itself on load.

**Live:** https://studio-echoframe.vercel.app · **Watch:** [YouTube](https://youtu.be/JTX3tQtxyhA) · **Video file:** [studio-echoframe.mp4](studio-echoframe.mp4) · **Code:** [studio-echoframe-code.html](studio-echoframe-code.html)


## 3. Café Chantilly: immersive café website
A live cocoa-and-cream swirl stirs under your cursor. The menu searches as you type, filters eggless, vegan, gluten-free and nut-free in one tap, and sorts by Desserts, Bakery, Beverages and Coffee. The About page has a live opening-hours table that highlights today.

**Live:** https://cafe-chantilly.vercel.app · **Watch:** [YouTube](https://youtu.be/mg2l3hoNsIg) · **Video file:** [cafe-chantilly.mp4](cafe-chantilly.mp4) · **Code:** [cafe-chantilly-code.html](cafe-chantilly-code.html)


## 4. Sandeep Organics: B2B chemical website

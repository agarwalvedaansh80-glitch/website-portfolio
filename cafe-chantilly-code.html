About Chantilly

A pâtisserie and coffee bar on Pali Hill, Bandra West.

Our story

Chantilly began as a neighbourhood idea: bake in small batches, keep the menu honest and seasonal, and serve cups that feel like comfort. Tres leches that soak just right, cheesecakes with clean finishes, brioche french toast on weekends, and hot chocolates that feel like a hug with coffee made carefully, not fussily.

We're proudly local in Bandra West: one cosy spot, crafted with care, and we keep things simple: clear labels, calm availability updates, and friendly service.
